A one-line confession from a Wi-Fi router, in its own voice, explaining why the connection dropped right at the movie's climax: after four years without a dusting, its fan gave up mid-sentence — and honestly, it was time.

## Entry

> I dropped the connection at the climax because after four years without a dusting, my fan gave up mid-sentence, and honestly, it was time.

138 characters, one line, one sentence. Written by `main.py` (stdlib only, deterministic) to `/output/entry.txt`, with a self-check that the entry is non-empty, a single line, at most 200 characters, pure ASCII, and exactly one sentence — exiting non-zero on any violation.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
